About the 2026 call

A foundation for your neuroscience career

The HOWLADER Rising Stars Fellowship supports undergraduates in Bangladesh who aspire to careers in neuroscience. For the 2026 cohort, we are again inviting applications from third- and fourth-year (or equivalent semester) students across the biological, biomedical, and engineering sciences.

Beyond a monthly stipend, fellows receive one-on-one mentorship on graduate applications and career planning. The aim is to help you build a strong record for admission to leading PhD programs worldwide.

Who can apply

Eligibility

Read each requirement carefully before applying. If you are unsure whether you qualify, reach out before the deadline.

  • ✓Currently enrolled as an undergraduate at an institution in Bangladesh.
  • ✓In your third or fourth year (or equivalent semester) at the time of application.
  • ✓A demonstrated aspiration to build a research career in neuroscience or a closely related field.
  • ✓Studying in one of the eligible fields listed below.

Eligible fields of study

Computer Science Chemical Engineering Materials Science Engineering Genetic Engineering Biomedical Engineering Biochemistry Microbiology Other related biological & biomedical sciences

What fellows receive

A comprehensive support system

💰

Financial support

A monthly stipend of 4,000 BDT for one full year, so you can invest your time in research and academics without financial burden.

🧭

Personalised mentorship

One-on-one guidance on graduate school applications, career planning, and research direction from an active neuroscientist.

How to apply

The application process

Confirm your eligibility

Make sure you meet every requirement above, including your year of study and field.

Prepare your materials

Have your CV, academic transcript, and a short statement of interest ready. In your statement, describe your motivation for neuroscience and your research goals.

Submit your application

Complete the application form before the deadline.

Interview & selection

Shortlisted candidates will be invited for a conversation. Selected fellows are then notified and onboarded into the program.
